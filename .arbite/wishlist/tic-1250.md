## Description
Wishlist item, captured from the request: "When hovering over a relationship line or group of relationship lines, visually indicate the nearest line under the cursor. Double clicking should teleport the view to the target of that line."

Analysis: most of the machinery already exists, so this is wiring rather than new infrastructure. `edgesNearPoint(scene, worldPoint, radius, limit)` in web/src/canvas/scene.ts (shipped for the near-pointer connection summary, tic-f1d7) already finds the edges within a constant screen-pixel radius of the pointer, nearest first, over the culled visible scene. Existing highlight plumbing (`connectionEdgesIncidentTo`, `highlightedEdgesLast`, the accent colours in web/src/canvas/theme.ts) shows how a lit line is drawn and ordered, and the camera flight behind goto (Workspace.tsx `onGoto`, tic-bee0) is the "teleport" -- a double-click on a line can reuse that flight to centre the line's `to` endpoint. What is missing: visually distinguishing the single nearest line under the cursor when a node is not under the pointer, and a double-click gesture on that line triggering the flight.

Acceptance criteria:
- Hovering empty canvas near a relationship line visually indicates the nearest line (constant screen-size pick radius; start from EDGE_HOVER_RADIUS_PX in web/src/settings.ts and tune), without disturbing node hover/selection behaviour.
- Over a bundle of near-overlapping lines, the nearest line is the one indicated; the existing near-pointer popup may still summarise the rest, but one line reads as "the one under the cursor".
- Double-clicking that line flies the camera to the line's target (its `to` endpoint), reusing the existing goto flight and its drag-override handling; an edge without resolvable endpoints no-ops.
- The nearest-line indication clears when the pointer moves away, over a node, or during pan/drag/marquee, and on any viewport change -- same gates as the tic-f1d7 popup.
- Frame rate stays smooth sweeping the pointer over the full ~190-file graph.

Classification note: reclassified from a raw `wish` to `feature` per the wishlist flow; filed in .arbite/wishlist/ until deliberately promoted to real work. Epic viz-modes-next because it is mode-agnostic scene-geometry interaction, the same surface as tic-f1d7.
